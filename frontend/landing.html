Questions

Things people ask us.

Is the food actually cooked at home?

Yes. Every cook on Yummara cooks from their own home kitchen — not a shared commercial space. That's the whole point.

How do I know it's hygienic?

Every kitchen is FSSAI-registered and onboarded by us before it goes live. Cooks who don't meet the bar don't get listed.

When should I order?

Order by 6 am for lunch tiffins. Outside that, you can order anything a cook has marked available right now.

How long does delivery take?

Most cooks are within a few kilometres of you. Prep time is shown on each kitchen, and a rider is dispatched the moment your food is ready.

Does my tip really reach the cook?

All of it. We don't take a cut of tips — what you add goes to the person who cooked your meal.

I cook at home. How do I join?

Tap Cook login and register with your phone number. You'll set up your menu, area and pickup address, and can go live once verified.
